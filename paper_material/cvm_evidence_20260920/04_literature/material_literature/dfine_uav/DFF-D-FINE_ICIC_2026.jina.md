Title: DFF-D-FINE: A Dual-Focus Fusion and Feature-Preserving D-FINE Framework for UAV Tiny Object Detection

URL Source: http://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-981-92-3378-6_36

Published Time: 2027

Markdown Content:
## Abstract

Tiny object detection in UAV imagery remains challenging because targets occupy only a few pixels, are densely distributed, and are easily confused by cluttered backgrounds. Existing detectors often lose tiny-object-sensitive cues at three stages of the feature pipeline: early extraction weakens shallow edge and texture details, conventional pyramid fusion insufficiently reinforces high-resolution representations, and standard downsampling further destroys fine structures. To address this issue, we propose DFF-D-FINE, an enhanced D-FINE framework for UAV tiny object detection. The framework combines a Hierarchical RepHMS-Enhanced Backbone to improve shallow feature representation, a Dual-Focus Pyramid Network to strengthen iterative high-resolution cross-scale fusion, and an Asymmetric Frequency-Spatial Downsampling module to preserve structural information during spatial reduction. On VisDrone2019, DFF-D-FINE achieves 24.7% AP and 42.0% AP 50, outperforming the D-FINE-S baseline by 4.1 and 6.1 points, respectively, while reducing parameters by 28.7%. On CODrone, it improves AP from 15.2% to 17.8% and AP 50 from 29.7% to 34.2%.
